A poultry farm has group of hens and cows need to find num of cows and hens separately, if total legs and total heads together give:
testcase 1: legs:16
	    heads:6
	    cows=2
	    hens=4
testcase 2: legs:7
	    heads:3
	    output: No Solution
code: heads = int(input("Enter number of heads: "))
      legs = int(input("Enter number of legs: "))
      for cows in range(heads + 1):
          hens = heads - cows
          if (4 * cows + 2 * hens) == legs:
             print("cows =", cows)
             print("hens =", hens)
             break
      else:
           print("No Solution")
